    Make a song with Music Blocks

    Music Blocks play a note when they scroll onto the screen. Where you put them decides the song.

    1 Place a Note Block

    Find the Note Block in the Terrain parts and put one in your course.

    2 Drop something on it

    Drag an item or enemy onto the Note Block. It turns into a Music Block. What you drop picks the sound.

    3 Higher block, higher note

    Each row up makes the note one small step higher. Row 1 is the very bottom row of your course.

    4 Keep the screen moving

    Space blocks out across the course. Use auto-scroll so the screen moves at a steady speed and the song keeps its beat.

    What you drop picks the sound

    Goomba
    piano
    Super Mushroom
    8-bit beep
    Bowser Jr.
    saxophone
    Koopa Clown Car
    synth chords

    Other items and enemies have their own sounds too. Try them all and pick a favorite.

    Song helper

    Pick a song and listen. The map shows where each block goes in your course.

    Each colored block is one Music Block. Columns are tiles going right. Rows are rows up from the bottom.

    BlockTile acrossRow up

    Too fast or too slow in the game? Change the tiles between beats, or try a different auto-scroll speed.

    Make up your own tune

    Tap squares to add notes. Listen, change it, and make it yours. Then use the build list to put it in your level.

    BlockTile acrossRow up

    Pro tip: in tall (vertical) areas the notes start over from low every 28 rows.
